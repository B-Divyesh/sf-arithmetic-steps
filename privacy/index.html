Plain-language policy

Privacy

Short version: Arithmetic Steps has no accounts, ads, trackers, or child profiles. Problems are stored only on the device unless you export them.

What is stored

When a problem is completed, its numbers, reasoning steps, and completion date are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. An unfinished problem also has a local browser checkpoint so it can survive an immediate refresh. We do not ask for or store names, ages, schools, email addresses, or other identifiers.

Where it goes

Problem data does not leave the browser. Export creates a JSON file only when you press “Export JSON.” Import reads the file you choose and writes its valid problems into this browser.

Network and analytics

The installed app caches its own files for offline use. This version contains no analytics, advertising, third-party scripts, hosted fonts, or behavioral tracking. A hosting provider may keep standard short-lived request logs for security and reliability.

Your controls

Use Saved problems to export or clear finished problems. You can also remove the site’s local data through your browser settings. Clearing browser storage removes unfinished work, saved problems, and offline files.

Children and contact

The product is designed to be used with a parent or teacher and deliberately avoids child identifiers. Questions about this policy can be opened in the project’s public source repository.
