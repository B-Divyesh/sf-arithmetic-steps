# Sandbox-verifiable pedagogy rules

These are observable product rules, not evidence of learning outcomes or an
outside endorsement.

1. A child can choose each chunk. Direct moves and labelled keyboard controls
   both make the same change. See `@claim:direct-manipulation` and
   `@claim:keyboard-controls`.
2. Every chosen chunk records an accurate equation and plain-language sentence.
   `@claim:narrated-steps` completes the final `52 − 18` chunk and checks its
   ledger, replay, and discussion-card wording. Unit coverage also checks
   `100 − 100 = 0`.
3. A finished route can be replayed with discussion prompts. See
   `@claim:replay-and-discussion`.
4. The adult-facing checklist checks the supplied route, a child-chosen chunk,
   reasoning, and keyboard access. Its marks are not stored. See
   `@claim:facilitator-checklist`.

This self-guided checklist is guidance, not evidence of learning outcomes.

The exact public claims and their sandbox commands are in
`.factory/claims.json`. `@claim:self-guided-checklist-guidance` checks this
boundary across product claim surfaces and rejects unsupported endorsement,
validation, study, and learning promises.
