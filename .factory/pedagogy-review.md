# Pedagogy review status

Checked 2026-08-29 for Arithmetic Steps.

## Product evidence

- The exercise stays within the brief's addition/subtraction-to-100 scope.
- Learners directly drag labelled one-counters and ten-frames, or use the
  equivalent labelled chunk controls without dragging.
- Learners choose chunks instead of entering a final answer; every movement is
  narrated and replayable.
- The adult note asks an open question before offering a strategy.
- Routes have no timer, score, streak, or leaderboard.
- Automated coverage verifies arithmetic boundaries, direct manipulation,
  keyboard parity, narrated steps, replay, discussion prompts, saved-data
  privacy, and reduced motion. Automation does not replace a pedagogy review.
