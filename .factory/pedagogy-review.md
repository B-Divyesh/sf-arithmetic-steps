# Qualified elementary-teacher pedagogy review

## Release status

**Pending external review — do not treat this product as classroom-release
ready.** The researched brief requires a qualified elementary-teacher review.
No such review is recorded in this repository as of 2026-09-05.

The self-guided facilitator checklist and automated regressions are useful
product checks. They do not replace an educator's review or establish a
learning outcome.

## Product evidence prepared for review

- Addition and subtraction use whole numbers with results from 0 to 100.
- Learners can drag labelled one-counters and ten-frames, or use labelled
  keyboard controls for the same moves.
- Learners choose chunks instead of entering an answer snapshot. Each move
  adds an equation and plain-language narration.
- Finished attempts replay and include discussion prompts.
- The activity has no timer, score, streak, leaderboard, answer guessing, or
  AI grading.
- The demo is local and isolated. It stores no child identifiers.

Automated checks cover these observable behaviors. They cannot verify a
teacher's qualification, judgement, feedback, or release decision.

## Required review procedure

1. A qualified elementary teacher uses addition and subtraction routes with
   direct dragging and labelled keyboard controls.
2. The reviewer checks the route narration, replay, and discussion card.
3. The reviewer records feedback, required changes, and a follow-up decision
   below. Any required changes must be implemented and independently retested.
